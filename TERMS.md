# DoubleSlash Terms of Use

**Effective date:** 2026-09-10

These terms apply to the DoubleSlash desktop and Android clients. DoubleSlash
is invite-only peer-to-peer software. There is no DoubleSlash account and no
central service that hosts your messages.

The privacy policy is [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md)
([public URL](https://github.com/ConquerD/DoubleSlash/blob/develop/PRIVACY.md)).

## 1. Your identity, your invites

You generate an identity on your device. You decide who to invite. You are
responsible for the people you admit to a 1:1 session or a room, and for what
you send them.

## 2. Prohibited use

You may not use DoubleSlash to create, upload, or distribute:

- Child sexual abuse material, or any content that sexualizes minors
- Content that facilitates exploitation or abuse of children
- Illegal content under the laws that apply to you
- Malware, or files sent to compromise another person's device
- Harassment, threats, or non-consensual intimate imagery

Invite-only is not a licence to do those things in private.

## 3. No server-side moderation

Conversations are end-to-end sealed. DoubleSlash (the project) cannot read,
scan, or take down a message on someone else's device. Moderation on this
network is **local**:

- **Block** a peer on your device so they cannot message or call you here
- **Report** from the app (peer long-press, or a room's long-press menu) — that
  opens a report you send to the maintainers via GitHub or your own mail app
- **Leave** a room, or hide it from your list

If you believe you have encountered child sexual abuse material, report it in
the app **and** to the authorities in your country. In the United States that
includes [NCMEC's CyberTipline](https://report.cybertip.org/).

## 4. Files, rooms, and portals

A file you send is offered from your device, not stored by a DoubleSlash
server. A room is hosted ephemerally by a supernode you chose to trust for
transport, not for content. In-app portal pages are operator-hosted code
running inside the client; treat them as you would any page from that operator.

## 5. Age

DoubleSlash is not directed at children under 13. Do not invite a child under
13 to use it.
